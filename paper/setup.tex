\section{Experimental design}\label{sec:setup}

\paragraph{Pre-registration and statistics.} Hypotheses, metrics, seeds, thresholds and tests were committed before
the main experiments (\texttt{PREREGISTRATION.md}, commit \texttt{d134c5a}); deviations are in Appendix~\ref{app:dev}.
Confirmatory claims use a hierarchical bootstrap (training seeds, then test episodes; $10^4$ resamples) with
Holm--Bonferroni correction across the 13 confirmatory tests. Every model is a GPT-style causal decoder (pre-LN,
$d{=}128$, 4 layers, 4 heads, MLP width 512, learned positions, about 0.8M parameters), trained from scratch with AdamW and
three seeds on a 4\,GB laptop GPU. ``Residual stream $\ell$'' is the input to block $\ell{+}1$.

\paragraph{A: in-context prediction of linear systems.} $x\in\mathbb{R}^2$, $u\in\mathbb{R}$ ($p{=}3$), $\sigma{=}0.1$,
prior $A_{ij}\sim\mathcal{N}(0,0.4^2)$, $B_i\sim\mathcal{N}(0,0.5^2)$ truncated to $\rho(A)<0.95$, inputs $u_t\sim\mathcal{N}(0,1)$,
$T{=}48$; token $t$ is $(x_t,u_t)$, target $x_{t+1}$; fresh batches for 20k steps. References: the \emph{exact} Bayes
predictor (truncated posterior mean by Monte Carlo), ridge/RLS, OLS, tuned one-step GD~\citep{vonoswald2023transformers},
and zero. Excess risk is measured against the noiseless mean $\theta z_t$.

\paragraph{B: MuJoCo physics.} \emph{Pendulum}: point mass on a 0.5\,m rod, $m\sim U[0.5,2]$, damping $c\sim U[0.02,0.3]$,
$g\sim U[4,16]$, random torques; regression on $(u,\dot q,\sin q,1)$ contains the true model class. \emph{Pushed block}:
box on a plane, Coulomb friction $\mu\sim U[0.1,1]$, $m\sim U[0.5,3]$, random forces; about half the steps are stuck, so
stick--slip lies outside any linear-in-parameters model. \emph{Bouncing ball} (exploratory, added later): $m$, $g$ and
the contact damping ratio unknown. Control step $0.05$\,s, unobserved process noise, 60k training episodes, $T{=}48$.
Baselines: simulation with the true or nominal parameters, physics-feature RLS, a no-context MLP, and on the block a
structured identifier with the exact simulator.

\paragraph{C: in-context adaptive control.} $A=0.9I+\mathcal{N}(0,0.25^2)$ truncated to $\rho(A)<1.1$ (\CfracUnst\% of
plants are open-loop unstable), $B=r(\cos\phi,\sin\phi)$ with $\phi$ uniform (actuation direction and sign unknown),
$J=\sum_{t<48}\|x_t\|^2+0.1u_t^2+\|x_{48}\|^2$, $|u|\le5$. The transformer reads $(x_t,u_{t-1})$ and outputs $u_t$; it
never sees $\theta$ and has no identification loss. Baselines, tuned on separate plants: oracle finite-horizon LQR, the best
constant gain, certainty equivalence (CE), CE with decaying dither~\citep{simchowitz2020naive}, explore-then-commit, and
Thompson sampling. Test plants and noise are shared across controllers.
