\paragraph{What the transformer computes.} In all three settings the trained models are competent in-context system
identifiers: they match the exact Bayes predictor at short contexts, beat linear-in-parameters regression on contact
dynamics, and adapt within a few steps. Their \emph{form} of identification is not the one we pre-registered. There is
no linearly readable, causally used parameter vector in the residual stream (an MLP probe does recover part of it, so the
information is present nonlinearly). The context information that the prediction depends on sits in the key--value memory
of the last two blocks, and the model's query Jacobian, rather than any activation, is what matches the Bayes estimate.
For the interpretability of adaptive controllers, including RMA-style policies, the practical lesson is to probe the
\emph{function} (Jacobians) and the \emph{memory} (keys and values), not only the activations.

\paragraph{Pre-registration.} Hypotheses, thresholds and tests were committed to the repository's git history before
the main runs (commit \texttt{d134c5a}); this is an internal record, not an externally time-stamped pre-registration.
Its effect here was concrete: a residual probe with $R^2{=}\ARtheta$ and a significant but negligible gain over the truth
(H2b) could have been reported as ``the model represents its estimate''; the pre-registered thresholds and the causal
tests H3a/H3b rejected that, and the key--value account that replaced it is labelled exploratory throughout.

\paragraph{Limitations.} (i)~\emph{Scale.} The models have 0.2--3M parameters, the linear systems have at most 24
unknowns, and the MuJoCo systems have one degree of freedom. Whether the key--value account holds for large
robot policies is open. (ii)~\emph{Post-hoc mechanism.} The key--value account was formulated after the
pre-registered probes failed, on the same models. \PENDING{oos} (iii)~\emph{Seeds.} Three training seeds per model; the
cluster bootstrap over three seeds under-estimates between-seed variance, so the confirmatory $p$-values are conditional on
these seeds, and seed-level results are reported alongside. (iv)~\emph{Approximation floor.} The predictors stay a factor
\Aratio\ above the Bayes risk averaged over contexts (H1c rejected). \PENDING{scaleclaim} (v)~\emph{Control recipe.}
Pure closed-loop BPTT, as pre-registered, did not train on open-loop-unstable plants; the controller results are for a
DAgger warm start followed by cost fine-tuning, and \PENDING{cvar} (vi)~\emph{Baselines in MuJoCo.} Feature RLS is
linear in its parameters and cannot represent stick--slip; the structured identifier of \S\ref{sec:expb} is the
model-based reference there. (vii)~\emph{Authorship.} This is single-author independent work produced with substantial
AI assistance (see below); every number is regenerated by the public scripts.
