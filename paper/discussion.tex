\paragraph{What the transformer computes.} In all three settings the trained models are competent in-context system
identifiers, but their \emph{form} of identification is not the one we pre-registered: there is no linearly readable,
causally used parameter vector in the residual stream (an MLP probe recovers part of it, so the information is present
nonlinearly). The sufficient statistics of the context sit in the key--value memory of the last two blocks, where they are
read with $R^2{=}\AcFwMom$ against at most \AcResBestMom\ from the residual stream (query, all positions, mean-pooled or last context token), and the model's
query Jacobian, rather than any activation, is what matches the Bayes estimate.
For interpretability of adaptive policies, probe the \emph{function} (Jacobians) and the \emph{memory} (keys
and values), not only the activations.

\paragraph{Pre-registration.} It is the commit tagged \texttt{prereg-d134c5a}, not externally time-stamped (the
Zenodo archive, doi:10.5281/zenodo.24031898, is later). Its thresholds and causal tests rejected reading a probe with
$R^2{=}\ARtheta$ as ``the model represents its estimate''.

\paragraph{Limitations.} (i)~\emph{Scale.} The models have 0.2--3M parameters, the linear systems have at most 24
unknowns, and the MuJoCo systems have one degree of freedom. Whether the key--value account holds for large
robot policies is open. (ii)~\emph{Post-hoc mechanism.} The key--value account was formulated after the
pre-registered probes failed, on the same models; a second pre-registration then confirmed it on three other architectures
from the same pipeline (\S\ref{sec:scale}), so other tasks remain open. The bouncing-ball system, trained after the account was formed, reproduces the inert residual probes but supports the key--value localisation only for one of three parameters (\S\ref{sec:expb}). (iii)~\emph{Seeds.} Three training seeds per model; the
cluster bootstrap over three seeds under-estimates between-seed variance, so the confirmatory $p$-values are conditional on
these seeds, and seed-level results are reported alongside. (iv)~\emph{Approximation floor.} The predictors stay a factor
\Aratio\ above the Bayes risk averaged over contexts (H1c rejected). Tripling the training steps or doubling the depth brings it to about \ScLongRatio, not to 1 (one seed each). (v)~\emph{Control recipe.}
Pure closed-loop BPTT, as pre-registered, did not train on open-loop-unstable plants; the controller results are for a
DAgger warm start followed by cost fine-tuning on a log objective; fine-tuning on the raw cost or on its CVaR diverged. (vi)~\emph{Baselines in MuJoCo.} Feature RLS is
linear in its parameters and cannot represent stick--slip; the structured identifier of \S\ref{sec:expb} is the
model-based reference there. (vii)~\emph{Authorship.} Single-author work with substantial AI assistance (see below).
